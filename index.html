Students managing real capital, for real community impact.

Mamaroneck Student Capital is a student-run investment fund, mentored by finance professionals, that channels classroom economics into real portfolio experience and charitable giving.

Our Mission Meet the Team

How the fund works

PORTFOLIO

Real capital, real stakes

Student Investment Club members research, pitch, and manage an actual portfolio, not a simulation.

MENTORSHIP

Guided by professionals

An adult board with professional finance experience reviews every pitch and oversees the fund.

IMPACT

Community impact

Like an endowment, a portion of the fund is donated annually to student activities and local charities.
